# Privacy Policy for YourLevel

**Last updated: 22 July 2026**

YourLevel ("we", "us", "the app") is a habit and task tracker. This policy
explains what data we collect, why, and your rights. Questions:
**mikedeboermario@gmail.com**.

## Who we are

YourLevel is operated by Mike de Boer (the "developer"), based in the
Netherlands. The app is distributed through the Apple App Store.

## What we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the app:

- **Account information** — when you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive
  your email address and (if provided) your name. You may set a display name
  and an optional profile picture.
- **App content you create** — your tasks, bad habits, XP, levels, streaks,
  daily logs, selected theme and decoration, day-start time, and referral
  code. This is what makes the app work and is tied to your account.
- **Subscription status** — whether you have an active "Pro" subscription and
  its expiry date. Payments are processed by Apple; we never see or store your
  card or payment details.
- **Profile picture** — if you choose to set one, the photo you pick is
  uploaded and stored so it can be shown in the app. We only access your photo
  library when you tap to choose a picture.
- **Anonymous usage analytics** — we record basic in-app events (for example:
  app opened, a task completed, a level reached, the paywall viewed) to
  understand how the app is used and improve it. This is tied to your account
  id but is **not** used to track you across other apps or websites, and is
  **not** used for advertising.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, browsing history, or device
advertising identifiers. We do **not** show ads. We do **not** sell your data.

## How we use it

- To provide and sync your account and progress across sessions and devices.
- To operate features like leveling, streaks, themes, and subscriptions.
- To send you local reminder notifications (only if you enable them; these are
  scheduled on your device).

## Who processes your data

We use trusted third-party services ("processors") to run the app:

- **Supabase** — hosts our database and handles authentication. Your account
  and app content are stored here. (https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **RevenueCat** — manages subscription status. (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- **Apple** — App Store distribution, Sign in with Apple, and payment
  processing. (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- **Google** — Sign in with Google, if you choose it. (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **PostHog** — anonymous product analytics (in-app events). (https://posthog.com/privacy)

These providers process data on our behalf to deliver the service.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can **delete your account and
all associated data at any time** from within the app: Settings → Account →
Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, tasks, bad habits,
daily logs, and referral data. You can also reset your progress via
Settings → Start Over.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA under GDPR), you may have the
right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can delete
everything in-app as described above, or contact us at the email above for any
other request.

## Children

YourLevel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal data from children under 13.

## Security

We rely on industry-standard measures from our processors (encrypted
connections, access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100%
secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the
"Last updated" date at the top.
