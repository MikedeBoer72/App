## Frequently asked questions

**How does leveling work?**
Complete your daily tasks to earn XP. You can earn up to 300 XP per day from
tasks, plus a bonus for every day of your streak. Each level takes a fixed
amount of XP, so every day you show up moves you forward.

**Why did I lose XP?**
Marking a bad habit as done costs you 150 XP. That's the point — the cost is
what makes tracking them honest.

**What is a streak?**
Finish all of your tasks for the day with no bad habits logged and your streak
grows by one. Streaks unlock bonus XP, and every 7 clean days bank a Streak
Freeze that protects you from a single missed day.

**When does my day reset?**
At the time you chose in Settings → Day Starting Time. Your tasks clear and
your streak updates then. You can change it twice a month.

**What do I get with YourLevel Pro?**
Pro lets you keep leveling past Level 5 and unlocks every theme at its
milestone level. It's an auto-renewing monthly subscription.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open the Settings app on your
iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then YourLevel. Deleting the app
does not cancel a subscription.

**I paid but the app still says Free.**
Open Settings → Subscription in the app and tap **Restore purchases**. If it
still doesn't appear, email us with the Apple ID email you purchased with.

**How do referrals work?**
Share your code from Settings → Refer Friends. When someone signs up using
your code and later subscribes to Pro, a free month of Pro is added to your
account.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your
profile, tasks, bad habits and history. To only reset your progress and keep
the account, use Settings → Start Over instead.

**Do you have an Android version?**
Not yet. iOS first — Android is on the roadmap.
